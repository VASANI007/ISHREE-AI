# 🚀 About ISHREE AI

ISHREE AI is a next-generation **Personal AI Operating System** designed to become an intelligent digital companion capable of understanding, reasoning, planning, communicating, and performing real-world tasks across desktop, mobile, browser, and cloud environments.

Unlike a traditional chatbot, ISHREE AI is built around a **Multi-Agent Architecture**, allowing specialized AI agents to collaborate and complete complex tasks autonomously.
